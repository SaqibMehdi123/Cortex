02Workflow

A loop, not a graveyard of notes.

Most tools stop at storage. Cortex closes the loop from first thought to finished work — scroll and watch it run.

  1. STEP 01

    Capture

    Clip it the second it appears. Quick capture and ⌘K from anywhere — no context switch, no friction.

  2. STEP 02

    Understand

    Reader mode plus a copilot that has read everything you saved. Ask it, summarize with it, connect through it.

  3. STEP 03

    Retain

    Flashcards and mindmaps on a spaced-repetition schedule. Memory that compounds instead of rotting in a folder.

  4. STEP 04

    Act

    Plan the week, run the focus timer, and let the radar surface what needs your attention.
